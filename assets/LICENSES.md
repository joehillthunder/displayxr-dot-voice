`models/ambulance.glb` and `models/apple.glb` ship with an external `Textures/colormap.png` in
their kits; `tools/embed-textures.ts` packed it into the GLB. The content is unchanged.

The DisplayXR sample `butterfly.sog` is © World Labs and is **not** redistributable, so it is not
used here.

Adding an asset: put it under `models/` or `splats/`, add a `catalog.json` entry with `license`
and `source`, and add a row here. `npm test` checks every catalog entry has both.
